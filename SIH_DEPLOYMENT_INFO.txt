==================================================
        IDGUARD - SIH 2026 MVP DEPLOYMENT
==================================================

1. ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
------------------------
- FRONTEND: Hosted permanently on Vercel. (React + Vite + TailwindCSS)
- BACKEND: Hosted locally on your PC. (Python + FastAPI + PyTorch + PaddleOCR)
- TUNNEL: Cloudflare Tunnels (routes internet traffic from Vercel to your laptop)

2. WHY THIS SETUP?
------------------
Our AI models (YOLOv8, InsightFace, PaddleOCR) are extremely powerful and require about 2GB of RAM to process Identity Cards and Faces. 
Free cloud providers (like Render or Hugging Face) restrict memory to 512MB, which causes the AI models to crash.
Therefore, using your laptop as the server during the hackathon demo is the most robust, fastest, and 100% free solution!

3. HOW TO START THE PROJECT FOR A DEMO
--------------------------------------
Step 1: Open the "E:\SIH\SIH2026" folder on your computer.
Step 2: Double-click the file named "START_IDGUARD_BACKEND.bat"
Step 3: Two black terminal windows will open. One is your Python API, the other is Cloudflare.
Step 4: Look closely at the Cloudflare terminal window. You will see a line that looks like this:
        https://<some-random-words>.trycloudflare.com
Step 5: Copy that exact URL.
Step 6: Go to your Vercel Dashboard -> IDGUARD -> Settings -> Environment Variables.
Step 7: Edit the value of "VITE_API_BASE_URL" and paste your new Cloudflare URL. Click Save.
Step 8: Go to Vercel Deployments -> Click the 3 dots (...) on the top deployment -> Click Redeploy.
Step 9: Open your live Vercel Website URL. Your MVP is now live and fully connected!

** Keep the terminal windows OPEN on your laptop as long as you want the website to work! **
